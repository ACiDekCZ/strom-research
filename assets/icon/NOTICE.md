# Strom Research icon

The tree of the Strom app (colour `#3f6b4f`) with a magnifying glass (`#f2b544`): Strom Research is
the research that fills the Strom app's family tree, and its shortcut is not to be taken for the app.

- `icon.svg` — the source (a white tree and an amber glass on a green rounded square);
  `icon-256.png`, `icon-512.png` (`rsvg-convert -w 512 -h 512 icon.svg -o icon-512.png`),
  `icon.ico` (16, 32, 48 and 256 px, PNG inside) — the desktop shortcut on Linux, macOS and Windows.
- `favicon.svg` — the outline for a web page (transparent).

The tree and the magnifying glass are drawn after the Lucide icons `tree-deciduous` and `search`
