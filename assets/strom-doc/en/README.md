# How Strom works

[Čeština](../cs/README.md) · **English** · [Deutsch](../de/README.md) · [Polski](../pl/README.md)

What Strom Research (the agent and the `strom` command) and the Strom app base their decisions on: the rules, the exact
limits, the order, and where each is controlled. For genealogists and experienced researchers. Every chapter and every
rule has a permanent number that can be quoted (for example R2.3). Terms are explained in the
[index of terms](../REJSTRIK.md); all numbers are in the [register of numbers](../CISLA.md).

Parts R (the research), A (the app) and S (the app and the research together) are approved and their numbers are permanent.
